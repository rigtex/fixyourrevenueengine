About

The operator behind the practice.

Revved for Growth is led by Jason Rigolli: two-time co-founder and CRO with over two decades rebuilding revenue engines on both sides of the founder journey.

Jason Rigolli
Founder, Revved for Growth
Connect on LinkedIn →

Jason has spent over two decades inside revenue functions, building scalable, repeatable growth engines for founder-led or family-owned B2B firms in the $2M–$15M range. He understands the founder journey firsthand: he co-founded, scaled, and exited two businesses early in his career before moving to the operator side.

Most of his work since has been inside the business, not on top of it. He's been hired by founders, CEOs, and managing partners to step into stalled revenue engines and rebuild how growth actually gets produced, across marketing, sales, and customer success. Where most fractional CROs sit in the deck, Jason sits in the engine: diagnosing where the system is breaking, owning the redesign, and operating alongside the team until the curve bends back upward.

The work shows up in measurable places. 205% of revenue targets at a stalled FinTech SaaS. 41–75% higher conversion across $5M–$12M B2B firms. 33–67% margin growth in founder-led and PE-influenced scale-ups. None of which came from another playbook; it came from someone willing to be in the room until it worked.
