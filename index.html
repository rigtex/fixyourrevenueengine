Built for the Stalled Scale Founder

Your revenue problem isn't marketing. It's system fragmentation.

At $2M–$15M, the business has grown past instinct but hasn't reached the structure that makes growth predictable. Marketing, sales, and customer success run in parallel lanes that never quite hand off. The business runs the founder, not the other way around — and there is never a quiet week to actually fix the system.

Hiring and firing sales staff hasn't fixed it. Agencies haven't fixed it. Tactical changes deliver temporary lifts and then revert. The problem isn't effort. The problem is that nobody is rebuilding the engine while the business is still flying.

Three ways to work together

Where is the revenue engine today?

Different stages of revenue maturity need different work. We operate across the full lifecycle — meeting the business where it actually is, not where a productized package wants it to be.

Fix

For stalled $5M–$15M firms where marketing, sales, and customer success are misaligned and growth has flattened. We get inside the system, find where the engine is leaking revenue, and repair the structural gaps — so the curve bends back upward without another round of hiring and firing.

Rebuild

For firms whose revenue engine has outgrown the design that got them here. We redesign how marketing, sales, and customer success produce growth together — turning inconsistent performance into predictable, measurable, repeatable revenue that the founder can finally see and control.

Build

For earlier-stage firms that need structure, not experimentation chaos. We install the revenue engine from zero — messaging, motion, measurement — so the next dollar of growth lands on a foundation designed to scale, instead of one being held together by the founder's calendar.
